# Zenith V3 — image sources

All photography used by the public site is real stock photography downloaded under the Unsplash License. None of the vessel, helm, antenna, wiring, dock, or electronics details were generated by AI.

## Technical graphics

The NMEA 2000 backbone, helm-installation, and VHF/DSC inspection graphics are hand-authored inline SVG. They are intentionally schematic and use labeled, plausible marine-system topology rather than generated pseudo-technical imagery.
