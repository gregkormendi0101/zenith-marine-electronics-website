Before scheduling

What to send with your request

Send enough detail to tell whether the problem fits Zenith's scope and what the first visit should accomplish.

  • Your name, phone, and email
  • Boat make, model, and length
  • Marina or dock location
  • What's on the network - chartplotter, autopilot, engine data, instruments
  • Anything you've noticed - dropouts, alarms, blanks (not required; the check is a health read, not a fault hunt)

Scope note: The check covers the NMEA 2000 network only. It is not fault-specific troubleshooting and not a repair: hunting down one named fault is diagnostic work, quoted separately before anything starts. Findings are measured results - never a parts recommendation tied to any shop.

Straight answers

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The check, the visit, and the written health note cost nothing and carry no obligation. It is how I meet boat owners and prove the work is measured, not guessed.

What happens if the check finds a problem?

You get the finding in writing, with what I would quote to put it right. Fault-finding and repairs are separate, quoted work - nothing starts without your go-ahead.

Do you need to open up my installation?

No. The check is read-only: one connection to a backbone tee, measurements and scans only. Nothing is opened, moved, or reconfigured.

Which boats and areas?

Boats with an NMEA 2000 network in Key Largo, Tavernier, and Islamorada. Checks are batched by marina into health-check days.
