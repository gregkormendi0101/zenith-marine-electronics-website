Problem first

Symptoms worth testing instead of guessing

A radio that powers on is not necessarily transmitting, receiving, identifying, or sharing position correctly.

  • Short or inconsistent transmit and receive range
  • DSC position or identity data is missing or incorrect
  • AIS targets disappear, own-vessel data is wrong, or alarms persist
  • Noise, weak audio, voltage drop, or intermittent resets occur
  • Coax, antenna, GPS, or network connections have unknown condition

Before scheduling

Useful information before the visit

Send enough detail to tell whether the problem fits Zenith's scope and what the first visit should accomplish.

  • Boat location, make, model, length, and operating area
  • VHF, AIS, antenna, splitter, GPS, and display makes and models
  • Exact symptoms, alarms, range concerns, or failed functions
  • Recent electrical, antenna, software, or network changes
  • Clear photos of radio, antenna base, coax connections, and related displays

Scope note: Non-GMDSS compulsory ship-radio inspection inquiries are screened separately for vessel applicability, equipment scope, calibrated-test requirements, and independence before scheduling. No statutory sign-off is implied by a general service request.

Can you configure DSC and AIS data sharing?

Yes, when the connected equipment supports the required GPS, identity, network, and configuration functions.

Potential non-GMDSS inspection work is screened before scheduling for vessel applicability, scope, calibrated equipment, and independence requirements.
