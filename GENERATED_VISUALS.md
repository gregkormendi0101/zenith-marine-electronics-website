These are conceptual system visualizations, not equipment-manufacturer diagrams. Literal technical claims are carried by adjacent semantic HTML labels and copy.

## NMEA network system

- Source: https://v3b.fal.media/files/b/0aa67a1e/FDMhOpwAAqJltvxcg4b60_w2uNmGKx.png
- Production: `assets/renders/nmea-system-1200.webp`, `assets/renders/nmea-system-2400.webp`

## Marine radio visual

- Source: https://v3b.fal.media/files/b/0aa67a0c/UIwHRW--8RDDUyhp4x8Tp_xt4oINAp.png
- Production crop: `assets/renders/radio-unit-1200.webp`, `assets/renders/radio-unit-2400.webp`
- Only the isolated channel-16 radio/control unit is used. Ambiguous source-image cable relationships, antenna and loose accessories were cropped out rather than presented as a literal installation.
- The accurate system architecture is a separate HTML diagram: protected 12 VDC and GPS position/MMSI feed the VHF/DSC radio; the RF output continues through 50-ohm coax to the antenna and on-air test.
