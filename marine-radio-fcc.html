What gets checked

A bright display does not prove the radio works.

  • Supply voltage, branch protection and grounding
  • MMSI, GPS position input and DSC behavior
  • Coax condition, connectors and antenna installation
  • Transmit and receive performance through the RF path
  • Required equipment, operation and records for covered vessels
