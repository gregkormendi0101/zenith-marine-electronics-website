Privacy policy

Last updated August 17, 2026

Zenith Marine Electronics uses limited website measurement to understand which services and outreach channels are useful. We do not sell personal information.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4. Google may process technical and usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, referral source, campaign parameters, and interactions with service, call, or text controls. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser storage. Google handles that data under its own privacy terms.

Booking and campaign attribution

The booking form creates a prepared text message on your device. The website does not submit or store the form itself. Nothing is sent to Zenith until you review the message and press send.

When a campaign or partner link is used, a short campaign attribution label may be included in the prepared text so Zenith can understand where the inquiry came from. The full page or browsing URL is not included.

Calls and text messages

Calls and SMS messages are handled by your carrier and Zenith’s communications provider, subject to their terms. Standard call or message rates may apply.

Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser, use browser privacy controls, or contact Zenith without using the booking form. Some analytics may not function when tracking protection is enabled.
